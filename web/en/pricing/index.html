Frequently Asked Questions

Are there extra commissions on the tickets you sell?

No, Ticketto takes no percentage commission on your tickets. You only pay the standard payment gateway fees (Stripe), and the revenue lands directly in your account.

Do I need to rent barcode scanners or handheld PDA hardware?

Absolutely not. Ticket check-in is done directly from a smartphone. Your staff just open the web dashboard on their phone and use the camera to scan QR codes in seconds.

Can I run online events (Zoom, Meet, Teams) with Ticketto?

Yes, on every plan — including Free. When you create the event, choose "Online" and add your meeting link: the "Join the Stream" button is included in the confirmation email and sent to the attendee only after registration (or payment) is confirmed, so the link never circulates beforehand.

Do I need special hardware to print badges at the entrance?

It depends on the plan. On Business, zero-tap thermal printing works with any Zebra or Brother printer that supports ZPL over the local network (port 9100): every time your staff scan a ticket, the badge comes out automatically, with no extra tap. On Pro, you can download an A4 PDF with 10 badges per sheet and print them at home with an ordinary office printer.

Can I customize the registration page with my logo and colors?

Yes, from the Pro plan up. Your organization's logo and name appear at the top of the public registration page (upload the image from your settings or paste an image URL) in place of the Ticketto logo, which stays only as a small footer signature. You can pick the event color from the presets or enter your brand's HEX code (e.g. #1B5E9E), or choose the minimal white style if you'd rather avoid colored backgrounds: text and buttons adapt on their own to stay readable.

Can I cancel my subscription at any time?

Yes, you can switch to the Free plan or cancel your subscription entirely on your own from your dashboard, with no long-term commitment.
