Fabio Gabelli

Founder of Ticketto

I'm Fabio Gabelli, founder of Ticketto. For over ten years I've worked at the intersection of software and events: I started out building digital tools for organizers and trainers, and over time I watched the same frustrations repeat dozens of times — queues at check-in, QR scanners that won't turn on, fees that eat the margin, attendees who can't find their ticket email.

I founded Ticketto to solve exactly these problems with a different approach: instead of selling dedicated hardware, we turn your staff's smartphone into a professional QR scanner. Instead of opaque fees, flat transparent pricing. Instead of a complex tool, a flow that lets you create an event in five minutes.

What I write here

On this blog I publish hands-on guides based on what we see every day working with hundreds of organizers: what works, what breaks the night, which mistakes cost you dearly. No generic theory — just practice and numbers.

  • Digital check-in and access control
  • Payments, GDPR and data privacy
  • Marketing and conversion optimization for registrations
