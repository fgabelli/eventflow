Features built for you

Developed alongside professional organizers to solve the real problems of live events.

🌐

Online or In-Person Events

One toggle: a physical address or a Zoom/Meet/Teams link. The streaming link is included in the email only after registration is confirmed — never before.

⏰

Time Slots

Automatically manage the flow of attendees by creating time slots with a maximum capacity each.

🖨️

Zero-Tap Thermal Printing

On the Business plan: connect a Zebra or Brother (ZPL) printer and the badge prints automatically with every scan — no extra tap from your staff.

🎨

Your Brand, Not Ours

On Pro and above: your registration page carries your organization's logo and name, in your brand colors (presets or a custom HEX code) or a clean minimal white style. Text stays readable with any color you pick.

📤

Bulk Import

Upload thousands of attendees in one go via CSV file. Save hours of manual data entry.

📄

Print-Ready PDF Badges

Export your entire guest list as an A4 PDF (10 badges per sheet) and print at home on an ordinary office printer. No thermal hardware required.

💌

Automated Communications

Ticketto automatically sends the confirmation email with the QR ticket and Apple/Google Wallet pass, plus a real-time notification to the organizer (Pro/Business).
