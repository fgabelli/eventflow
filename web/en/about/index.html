About Us

We're the team behind Ticketto, a platform built to solve the real problems event organizers face every day.

Our mission

Ticketto was built by Bitle S.r.l.s. with one clear goal: to take the tech headache out of running events. Far too often, organizers find themselves wrestling with outdated platforms, sky-high ticket fees and the need to rent expensive hardware just to scan people in at the door.

We've created an ecosystem where all you need is your smartphone to manage the flow of people through your door, and where the only fees you pay are the fair, transparent ones from the payment gateway (Stripe).
