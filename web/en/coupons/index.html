Frequently asked questions

What does the customer need to use a coupon?

Just a phone. They open the campaign link or scan the QR code on your poster, enter their details, and instantly receive a personal voucher with a unique code (for example SPA-0024) and a QR code. There's no app to install.

How is a coupon redeemed at the counter?

From the web dashboard you scan the customer's QR code with a phone camera or a barcode scanner. If they printed the code or read it out to you, you look it up manually and mark it as redeemed. Used and expired vouchers are blocked automatically, so the same coupon can't go around twice.

Can I collect payment online?

Yes — on the Pro plan and above you can charge online with Stripe at claim time, and the system keeps track of what was paid online versus on site. On the Free plan the promotion runs in "pay on site" mode: the customer claims the voucher and pays you in person.

Do I need a designer for the flyers?

No. Ticketto generates print-ready PDFs in four formats — A4 desk poster, A6 flyer, 85 × 55 mm business card, and an A4 sheet with six coupons and cut lines — with nine visual themes to choose from, your logo, an optional partner logo, and your contact details (address, phone, website and WhatsApp) in the footer. If you'd rather use your own artwork, you can download just the QR code as a PNG.

Can I run a promotion with another business?

Yes — that's exactly what partner offers are for: both logos appear on the printed material, so you can reserve your offer for another business's customers (a spa dedicating a treatment to a gym's members, say) and see how many of them actually turn up.

How many coupons can I issue?

On Free, one active campaign and up to 25 vouchers; on Pro, five active campaigns and up to 250 vouchers each; on Business, unlimited campaigns and vouchers. Limits apply to active campaigns: closing a promotion frees the slot.

How is this different from the events side of Ticketto?

They're two ways of using the same platform. With events you handle registrations and check-in for a specific date; with promotions you handle an offer the customer can use whenever they like, up to its expiry. Either way the outcome is the same: you know who showed up, with a name and a contact.
